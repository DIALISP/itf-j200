Tournament Information

The ITF J200 is part of the international ITF Junior Circuit, where top U18 players compete for world ranking points and develop their pathway toward professional tennis. The tournament attracts highly talented players from around the world and represents an important step in international junior competition.

The event takes place in Gladbeck, Germany, hosted by the Tennis-Club Haus Wittringen . The club is located in the scenic Wittringen park area, one of the most beautiful green recreational zones in the Ruhr region, offering a unique atmosphere surrounded by nature and historic surroundings near Schloss Wittringen.

The facility features 10 outdoor clay courts set in a quiet park environment, providing excellent playing conditions throughout the summer season. In addition, the club operates a nearby indoor facility with 3 covered courts, ensuring training and match play options regardless of weather conditions.

Established in 1947, the Tennis-Club Haus Wittringen has a long tradition in German tennis and is well known for its strong club culture, youth development, and its integration into the local community. The combination of history, modern facilities, and natural surroundings makes it an ideal venue for an international junior tournament.

Official Hotel

The official hotel and main sponsor of the tournament is the Van der Valk Hotel Gladbeck, providing premium accommodation for players, coaches, and tournament guests.

The hotel is located in close proximity to the tournament venue at the Tennis-Club Haus Wittringen , situated in the scenic Wittringen park area. The walking distance between the hotel and the tennis club makes it especially convenient for players, allowing easy access without the need for transportation.

Surrounded by green landscapes and recreational park areas, the route between the hotel and the club offers a calm and pleasant environment, reflecting the professional but relaxed atmosphere of the tournament.

As the main sponsor of the Van der Valk Junior Open, the hotel plays a key role in supporting the organization of the event and providing high-quality hospitality for international junior athletes.
